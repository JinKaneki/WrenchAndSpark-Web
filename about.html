About Wrench & Spark

Your trusted partner for professional-grade automotive tools and equipment since 2023

Our Story

Founded in 2023 by a seasoned master mechanic, Wrench & Spark was born from a simple yet powerful realization: quality automotive tools shouldn't break the bank, and every mechanic deserves access to professional-grade equipment regardless of their budget.

After 15+ years in the automotive industry, our founder Jin Kaneki grew frustrated with the lack of reliable, high-quality online retailers serving the South African market. Too often, mechanics and DIY enthusiasts had to choose between overpriced premium brands or cheap, unreliable alternatives that couldn't withstand professional use.

We decided to change that. By cutting out the middleman and sourcing tools directly from trusted manufacturers, we provide our customers with durability and performance at fair prices. Today, we serve mechanics across South Africa with an extensive catalog of hand tools, power tools, diagnostic equipment, and tool storage solutions.
